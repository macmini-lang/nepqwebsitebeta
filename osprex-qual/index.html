How We Keep Close Rates High

Operators who've tried other coaching usually say one of two things: "It worked at first then the team slipped back" — or — "It was too generic to actually stick." Common problems when sales coaching isn't done right:

  • ✕ Group calls with 100 people where your rep is invisible
  • ✕ Generic scripts that fall apart on a real objection
  • ✕ No daily accountability — reps slip back to old habits
  • ✕ Coaches who stopped doing deals years ago

Here's how we do it differently:

  • ✓ We review every single call your team has — daily audits, not weekly check-ins
  • ✓ 1-on-1 and small group sessions (~10 people) — your rep gets trained directly, not lost in a crowd
  • ✓ Rep-specific drills on their exact weakness — if they're struggling with objections, we drill that exact objection until it's locked in
  • ✓ Jon Lallande still runs his own deals and coaches live calls every week — not pre-recorded modules from someone who quit wholesaling
  • ✓ We identify your top 5 most-likely-to-close leads in week one and coach your reps to close them — quick win before we've changed anything else
